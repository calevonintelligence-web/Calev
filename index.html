About

About Calevon Intelligence

Calevon Intelligence Company is a global business intelligence and risk assessment firm able to research markets, companies and risks anywhere in the world. We support companies, investors and professional organizations that need dependable information before they commit capital, sign agreements or enter new markets.

Our work combines open-source intelligence (OSINT), geospatial intelligence (GEOINT) and human intelligence (HUMINT) gathered through lawful and transparent methods. We work with a network of vetted local partners and researchers across the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada, Europe, Latin America, the Middle East, Africa, Central Asia and the Asia-Pacific region, which gives clients both local insight and international perspective.

We operate under strict ethical standards: we comply with applicable laws and data protection rules in every jurisdiction, we never use unlawful surveillance or deception, and we protect the confidentiality of every client engagement.

Methodology

How we gather intelligence

Three complementary disciplines, each applied within legal and ethical limits.

OSINT

Open-source intelligence

Systematic collection and verification of publicly available information: registries, filings, media, publications and digital sources, cross-checked before it reaches a report.

GEOINT

Geospatial intelligence

Analysis of satellite imagery, maps and location data to confirm assets, infrastructure, activity and changes on the ground.

HUMINT

Human intelligence

Transparent, consent-based conversations with knowledgeable people and local experts, conducted openly and within the law.
